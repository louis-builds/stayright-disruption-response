# Cancellation & Rebooking Policy

## Free cancellation window
Most bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in; cancelling after that incurs a one-night fee. Exact terms follow what's on file for each hotel.

## Deferral due to a disruption
If a guest needs to defer their stay because of a platform-confirmed disruption (weather, flight, or road), the original hotel can approve the deferral with one click after confirming availability, with no rebooking fee. If the original hotel can't hold the room, the guest can choose an alternative stay or cancel for a refund.

## Alternative-stay price difference
Any price difference for an alternative stay is calculated automatically from the room rates on file for that hotel, and shown as an itemized breakdown. Anything not yet priced or confirmed is clearly marked "pending" — we never commit to a number in advance.

## Refund policy
Every cancellation refund must be manually confirmed by a platform coordinator, who records the amount, reason, operator, and time. The refund confirmation email is only sent after that confirmation — refunds are never issued automatically.

## No-show terms
A booking that isn't checked into on time, with no advance notice to the platform, is treated as a no-show and isn't covered by the free-cancellation or deferral terms above.
