# User Guide

## Viewing your disruption notifications
After signing in, your homepage shows a list of disruption notifications related to you, sorted newest first, with unread ones marked by a red dot. Click a notification to open the related conversation.

## Contacting a coordinator
When the system decides your case needs human help, the conversation will show a "Chat now" link — click it to open a dedicated conversation with a coordinator. You can also reopen the same conversation any time from your "To-do" list; history is never lost.

## Editing your profile
After signing in, click your avatar in the top right and choose "Profile" to update your avatar, nickname, gender, language, email, phone, and password. Changes take effect immediately.

## Language settings
The system supports English, Chinese, and Māori. You can switch language during registration or from your profile page; the support chat will reply in your chosen language.
