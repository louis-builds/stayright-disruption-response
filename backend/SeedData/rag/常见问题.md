# Frequently Asked Questions

## My booking has been affected by a disruption — what happens next?
As soon as the system detects that a disruption may affect your booking, we'll notify you (target: within 15 minutes) via in-app message and email, and in parallel contact your original hotel to ask about deferring or holding your room. You can open the conversation from the notification or your "To-do" list to see progress and available options.

## What options can I choose from?
Usually three options: defer and keep the original hotel, move to a nearby alternative hotel, or cancel with a refund. Which ones are available depends on hotel confirmation — the system only shows options that are confirmed, or clearly marked as pending confirmation.

## How long does a refund take?
All refunds must be manually confirmed by a platform coordinator before they're processed — nothing is paid out automatically. Once confirmed, you'll get an email with the refund result and expected arrival time.

## Can I book a new hotel directly on this platform?
No. This platform only handles notifications, rebooking, and cancellation/refunds for existing bookings affected by a disruption — it does not offer open public hotel booking.
